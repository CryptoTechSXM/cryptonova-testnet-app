How It Works
1

Price Discovery

Floor price = Treasury USDC ÷ circulating supply. You always buy above floor — treasury never loses backing.

2

Bonding Curve

Price tier rises with supply: 1.25× → 1.50× → 1.75× → 2.00× floor. Early buyers pay the lowest premium.

3

Premium Split

The amount above floor value is split dynamically between the Stability Fund and Liquidity Reserve based on each fund's deficit.
