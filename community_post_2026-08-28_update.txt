🚀 CryptoNova Update — what we fixed, and what you will see next

Hi team 👋

We have been heads down for a few days closing real problems that some of you
reported. Here is where we are, in plain language.

🔧 WHAT WE FIXED

✅ The referrer problem is solved.
Some of you told us that when you typed a sponsor address it "reverted back to my
original default address". You were right, and it was not a MetaMask bug — it was
ours. Two separate faults: switching accounts in your wallet without reloading kept
the first account's sponsor, and a correct address typed with the wrong upper/lower
case was rejected and quietly replaced. Both are fixed, and we proved it on chain
with a real registration before saying a word here.

✅ Pay It Forward waitlist now shows properly.
The list was being written to one place and read from another, so it looked empty
even when there were people on it.

✅ You can sponsor as many entries as you like.
The page used to say one active sponsorship at a time. That was never actually
enforced anywhere in the contract, so the page was telling you something untrue. We
removed it rather than pretend.

✅ The gas gift now goes where it should.
Every coupon still includes gas for the new member — that was YOUR idea and it stays.
It was landing in the wrong wallet. Rewired and confirmed on chain.

✅ Filing a bug report no longer blocks site updates.
Every report used to consume one of our daily deployment slots, so a busy bug day
could stop us shipping fixes. Member reports now go somewhere that does not compete
with releases. Keep them coming 🙏

🐞 BUG REPORT CLEAN-UP

We went through every single open report today — 17 of them. We closed 3 where we
could point at the exact code that fixes them, and left the rest open with a specific
next step written down for each.

We deliberately did NOT mass-close the old ones. It would have been easy to say "that
was on the old test deployment" and wipe the list clean. We closed one report that way
once before, the chain proved us wrong, and we had to reopen it. Not doing that again.

If your report is still open, it is still open on purpose. A few of you will hear from
us directly asking you to retest.

🛡️ THE METAMASK WARNING

Some of you saw a "malicious site" warning from MetaMask. That came from a security
scanner, and the cause was on us: during our last deploy a number of contracts had not
yet been verified on BaseScan, which is exactly the pattern those scanners flag.

All 46 contracts are now verified and public. We have submitted the de-listing appeal
with full documentation and we are waiting on their response. We will tell you the
moment it clears. 💪

📈 SOMETHING YOU WILL NOTICE — SIMULATED GROWTH

Starting today we are running a growth simulation on the test system. You will see the
member count climb steadily over the coming days.

Please read this part carefully 👇

Those are TEST accounts created by us, not a sudden rush of new people. We are doing
it on purpose, to load the matrices, the rescue system and the Stability Fund harder
than the community alone can right now — so that anything that breaks under real
growth breaks HERE, on testnet, where it costs nobody anything.

Your own positions, earnings and placement are not affected. If you notice anything
that looks wrong while this is running, that is exactly what we want to hear about.

💵 THE $1 BUG BOUNTY IS ALWAYS ON

Every valid bug report earns $1. It has never been switched off. The referrer fix
above exists because two members took the time to report it properly — that is worth
a lot more to us than a dollar.

Report anything through the Bug Report page on the site.

🙏 Thank you for testing with us and for being patient while we fix things properly
rather than quickly. We are in building phase, and being straight with you about what
is broken is how we intend to keep doing this.
