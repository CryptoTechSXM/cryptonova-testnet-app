🤝 COMMUNITY PROGRAM

Pay It Forward

A member-funded sponsorship program. Existing members cover the full cost of entry for people who cannot yet afford one — as a gift, not a loan. Nothing is repaid; recipients are simply asked to sponsor one entry themselves once their position begins to earn.

How it works
1

Request

Add your wallet address to the waitlist below. No wallet yet? The form explains how to create one, free of charge.

2

Receive

A member funds an on-chain coupon covering your full $10 entry and sends you the code. Registration costs you nothing.

3

Pass it on

There is no repayment. When your position starts earning, sponsor one entry for the next person on the list.

Request a sponsored entry

Submit your details below. Members review this list and choose who to sponsor — when a member funds your entry, you will receive a coupon code that covers your registration in full.

No wallet yet? Install MetaMask from your device’s app store and create a wallet — it is free — then paste its address here. When your coupon arrives, open this site inside the wallet app’s built-in browser to register.

🔒 Protect your wallet. When you create a wallet you will be shown a recovery phrase (also called a seed phrase) and a private key. These are your personal credentials — treat them like your online banking login. Never share them with anyone, and never type them into any website. Your sponsor only needs the wallet address (starting with 0x) — nobody from CryptoNova, no sponsor, and no administrator will ever ask for your recovery phrase or private key.
Sponsor an entry — for members

Your $10 funds an entry coupon on-chain. Choose a recipient from the waitlist below, or someone you know personally. The recipient registers free of charge and is placed in your referral line, so you grow together. One active sponsorship at a time — your next unlocks once the current coupon is redeemed.
