🔎 WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED
The app told you "transaction failed on chain". That wording was wrong, and we are sorry — nothing ever reached the blockchain.

What really happened is that the free public Base Sepolia node refused the request because it was busy. It answered with a "403 - too many requests". So the transaction was never sent, nothing was charged, and your wallet and your funds are completely fine.

Restarting your device could never have fixed it, because the problem was not on your side at all. That one is on us.

🔁 WHAT TO DO NOW
Wait a few minutes and press the button again. It clears by itself — this is the free test node getting busy on launch day, not a problem with your account or your registration.

If it keeps happening, try Rabby wallet. We have not seen this happen on Rabby so far. That is not a promise, just what we have seen.

🛠️ WHAT WE CHANGED
We fixed the message. From now on it will tell you the node was busy and to try again in a few minutes, instead of blaming the chain. Your two reports are what made us go and look.

📌 FOR MAINNET
Mainnet will run on a paid node with redundancy, so this class of error should not follow us there. On testnet we would rather you retry for free than pay for a node today.
