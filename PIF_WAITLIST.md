# PIF — Pay It Forward waitlist

People who raised their hand at /pif.html and are waiting for a gifted entry.
One line per person, appended by api/pif-request.js. [WAITING] flips to
[GIFTED by 0x... date] when a member gifts them a coupon. The coupon codes
themselves NEVER appear here — they live between gifter and recipient.
