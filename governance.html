How Governance Works
1

Hold CNOVA

Your vote weight equals your CNOVA balance at the time you vote. No staking, no lockup.

2

Propose

Any holder of at least 0.01% of supply can submit a param-change proposal. Only allowlisted values accepted.

3

Vote — 72 Hours

For or Against. Quorum = 2% of supply. Simple majority (>50% of votes cast) to pass.

4

Timelock — 48 Hours

Passed proposals enter a 48-hour timelock before anyone can execute. Community can review and respond.

5

Execute

After timelock, anyone can call execute. The parameter changes on-chain immediately. Expires if uncalled in 72h.
