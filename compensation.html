⛓️ Stream 2 — Chain Pay (6-Level Depth)

13.5%  split equally across 5 upline BFS positions (L2–L6) — 2.7% per level

Every single registration in the network — regardless of who referred them — sends 13.5% of the entry fee upward through 5 BFS levels (2.7% each to L2–L6). Combined with the 5% L1 referral bonus above, 18.5% of every entry flows upward. If someone joins anywhere below you in the tree, you earn automatically based on your BFS position. This is the passive engine of CryptoNova.

📍 Chain Pay follows the MATRIX, not your referral tree. The five 2.7% payments go to the members seated directly above the new member's matrix position — whoever they are. Your personal referrals pay you the 5% direct bonus (Stream 1); your referral's referral pays chain pay to their seat uplines, not automatically to you. In return, you receive chain pay from everyone who lands under your seat — referred by you or by anyone. Over time the matrix pays you from far more people than your personal tree alone.

🌀 Stream 3 — Equalization Pool (Continuous Cycle Pay)

18%  of every entry accumulates in the equalization pool — flat rate at all tiers

Every CryptoNova matrix holds 127 seats (a 7-level BFS tree). As each new member fills a seat, 37.9% of the 47.5% payout base flows into that matrix's equalization pool — equal to 18% of the full entry fee at every tier. Once the matrix is full, every new entry rotates it: the root cycles out, everyone advances a seat, and the pool distributes across all remaining seats — weighted by seat depth, so deeper seats receive larger shares. Your pool earnings accumulate rotation after rotation as you climb toward the root — a continuous drip, not a single jackpot.

Deeper seats earn the largest shares each rotation, which offsets the wait — that's the "equalization." Faster-filling matrices = more rotations = more frequent pool pay for everyone seated.

Cycle-out & re-entry: at MatA cycle-out you cross into MatB — the crossing costs the full entry fee, funded half by your crossing reserve and half by your accumulated earnings. At MatB cycle-out the V8.43 additive engine automatically re-enters and/or upgrades you from your accumulated funds (re-entry → upgrade → optional double seat) — no manual action required.

🪙 Stream 4 — CNOVA Token Mining

Minted to your wallet on every entry — halving epochs

CryptoNova mints CNOVA tokens to your wallet automatically each time a new entry is registered (at any tier). The amount follows a halving schedule — similar to Bitcoin. Joining early captures the highest per-entry reward before each halving kicks in.

Your CNOVA reward is always fully backed. A T1 entry sends $0.50 to the CNOVA Treasury and mints CNOVA against exactly that $0.50 — 50 CNOVA at the $0.01 launch floor, and proportionally fewer as the floor climbs. Nothing unbacked is ever minted, so joining the matrix can never push the floor down. Epoch halvings lower the scheduled reward on top of this. From epoch 2 the reward halves each epoch (40 → 20 → 10 → 5 → 2.5) before plateauing at 2.5 CNOVA for the remaining epochs. There are 9 epochs total — the last is the Final Frontier, formula-driven by the live floor price. An epoch advances on whichever of three triggers comes first — CNOVA minted, members joined, or 30 days elapsed — so the rate can step down even in a quiet month. All three are governance-adjustable.

📈 Stream 5 — CNOVA Floor Price Appreciation

5%  of every entry fee backs the CNOVA floor price

5% of every entry fee flows into the on-chain CNOVA Treasury (= 10.5% of the 47.5% payout base). The floor price is calculated automatically: floor = Treasury reserve ÷ CNOVA total supply.

The floor price rises as higher tiers add backing faster than they add supply, and as early-unlock penalty tokens are burned — burning cuts supply without touching the Treasury, so the backing per remaining token goes up. And because the protocol never mints CNOVA beyond what that entry's own Treasury deposit backs, new entries can never push it down. CNOVA holders can redeem for USDC at the floor price directly from the Dashboard, with no approval needed and no exit fee. The one constraint is vesting: matrix-minted CNOVA is cliff-vested for 180 days, and unlocking a batch early costs up to 50% of that batch, sliding to zero as its unlock date approaches. Both schedules are governance-adjustable.

About the Stability Rescue Fund
If your earnings can’t cover a re-entry, the Stability Rescue Fund can advance the shortfall so your position keeps moving. This is a repayable advance, not a gift. It is recorded against your account and repays itself automatically out of your future earnings — 60% to 90% of each payout depending on which tier issued it — across every tier you earn in. Upgrading a tier settles whatever is left as part of the upgrade. Your seats and earnings keep working normally throughout, and nothing is ever taken from your wallet.
