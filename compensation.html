🤝 Stream 1 — Direct Referral Bonus

10–20%  paid instantly on every entry

When someone joins using your referral link — or upgrades to a higher tier — your wallet receives a percentage of their entry fee instantly, on-chain, with no approval required. The rate is 10% at Tiers 1–3 and rises to 20% at Tier 4 and above. This relationship is locked permanently; every future upgrade they make keeps earning you the L1 bonus.

⛓️ Stream 2 — Chain Pay (6-Level Depth)

17–20%  split across 6 upline BFS positions

Every single registration in the network — regardless of who referred them — sends 17–20% of the entry fee upward through 6 BFS levels (the rate depends on tier). If someone joins anywhere below you in the matrix, you earn automatically based on how many BFS levels separate you. This is the passive engine of CryptoNova.

🌀 Stream 3 — Matrix Cycle Payout

22.5–44%  accumulates in the equalization pool — paid on cycle

Every CryptoNova matrix holds 127 seats (a 7-level BFS tree). As each new member fills a seat, a share of their entry fee flows into that matrix's equalization pool — 44% at Tiers 1–3, stepping down to 26% at T4–T5, 24.5% at T6–T7, and 22.5% at T8–T10 (higher tiers shift more of the fee into referral and chain pay instead). When all 127 seats are filled, the member at position #1 (root) receives the full pool and is automatically re-entered into the next active matrix.

You don't need to be the root to benefit. Being earlier in the queue means more entries land below you, generating chain pay throughout the fill. Faster-filling matrices = more frequent earnings for everyone in the queue.

Auto re-entry: the protocol automatically re-enters the cycling member into the next active matrix. A portion of the cycle payout funds your re-entry fee — no manual action required.

🪙 Stream 4 — CNOVA Token Mining

Minted to your wallet on every entry — halving epochs

CryptoNova mints CNOVA tokens to your wallet automatically each time a new entry is registered (at any tier). The amount follows a halving schedule — similar to Bitcoin. Joining early captures the highest per-entry reward before each halving kicks in.

T1 Genesis epoch starting reward: 50 CNOVA per $10 entry. From epoch 2, rewards halve cleanly every two epochs (40 → 20 → 10 → 5 → 2.5) before plateauing at 2.5 CNOVA. There are 9 epochs total — the last is the Final Frontier, formula-driven by the live floor price.

📈 Stream 5 — CNOVA Floor Price Appreciation

10%  of all entry fees back the CNOVA floor price

10% of every entry fee flows into the on-chain CNOVA Treasury. The floor price is calculated automatically: floor = Treasury USDC ÷ CNOVA total supply.

The floor price can only rise as more members join — more USDC flowing into Treasury relative to new CNOVA minted means the backing per token increases. CNOVA holders can redeem for USDC at the floor price at any time, directly from the Dashboard, with no approval or lock-up.

🏛️ Stream 6 — Community Wallet (First 1,000 Members)

Exclusive lifetime bonus for the first 1,000 wallets — paid monthly, for as long as you hold your spot

The 1% "Community Wallet" line in the entry-fee split below isn't just a protocol reserve sitting idle — every entry, from every member, at every tier, forever feeds this pool. What makes it special is who gets paid from it: only the first 1,000 wallets ever to register with CryptoNova are eligible to receive distributions, split into two permanent cohorts by join order.

Enrollment is automatic and permanent the moment you register — there's no separate sign-up, and it's capped at one slot per wallet address for life. Once the 1,000th member joins, the cohort is closed forever; no one can buy or earn their way into it after the fact.

How distributions work: every 30 days, anyone can trigger a distribution. Half of the pool's current balance pays out — 60% split evenly across all enrolled Genesis members, 40% split evenly across all enrolled Pioneer members. The other half rolls over, compounding the pool for next month. The pool is also topped up by orphan fees — entry fees that had no valid upline to pay and would otherwise be stuck.
