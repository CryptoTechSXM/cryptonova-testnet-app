🤝 NEW: PAY IT FORWARD IS LIVE

Hey team 👋

Today we launch something I am very proud of — Pay It Forward, our community sponsorship program. You will see the new 🤝 Pay It Forward tab in the menu right now.

💡 WHAT IT IS
Not everyone can afford the $10 entry — and every one of us got our start somewhere. Pay It Forward lets an existing member gift a full entry to someone who cannot yet afford one. It is a gift, not a loan. Nothing is repaid, ever. The only ask: when your position starts earning, gift one entry to the next person on the list.

🙋 NEED AN ENTRY?
Open the Pay It Forward page and add your wallet to the waitlist. When a member sponsors you, you receive a coupon code that covers your registration completely — you pay $0. No wallet yet? The page walks you through creating one for free.

💚 WANT TO SPONSOR SOMEONE?
Your $10 funds an entry coupon on-chain. Pick someone from the waitlist, or gift someone you know personally. Your recipient is seated IN YOUR LINE — you grow together. Sponsor as many entries as you like, and any unused coupon can be cancelled for a full refund. The page shows every coupon you have ever funded and exactly what happened to it.

🔒 STAY SAFE
Your sponsor only ever needs your wallet ADDRESS (starting with 0x). Nobody from CryptoNova — no sponsor, no admin — will EVER ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Never share them with anyone.

🐛 As always, the $1 bug bounty is active — see something off, hit the report button on any page and get paid.

Open the door. Hold it open for the next person. 🤝
