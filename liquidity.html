How It Works
01
Deposit USDC
Deposit any multiple of $10 (min $10). Your USDC stays in the smart contract — no lock-up, withdraw anytime.
02
Fees flow in
When a new member uses Transak or Ramp to fund their wallet, a small partner fee is routed to the pool automatically every 15 minutes.
03
Earn pro-rata
Your share of each fee distribution is proportional to your stake. $100 in a $1,000 pool = 10% of every distribution.
