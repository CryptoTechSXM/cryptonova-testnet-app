What is CryptoNova?
A complete Web3 ecosystem on Base Chain, run entirely by smart contracts — no middlemen, every payment on-chain and verifiable.
🔗 Matrix rewards engine — a 127-seat figure-8 matrix at every tier. Earn 5% on every direct referral plus automatic chain pay from anyone who lands under your seat — referred by you or not.
🪙 CNOVA token with a rising floor — every entry sends 5% into a USDC treasury that backs the token. The floor formula is on-chain: treasury ÷ supply. More members = higher floor — it can only move up.
💧 Staking & liquidity yield — stake USDC to earn a share of on-ramp partner fee revenue, or provide CNOVA/USDC liquidity and earn 0.30% on every swap.
🏛️ DAO governance — token holders co-govern the protocol through on-chain proposals.
🤝 Community Wallet — 1% of every entry pools for the first 1,000 founding members, distributed on the 25th of every month.
🛡️ Stability Fund — an automated rescue system that keeps every member's position moving; advances are repayable from your future earnings, so nobody gets left behind.
💵 Start with $10 — fully passive if you want (automation handles re-entry and upgrades for a small reserve), or bring 2–3 active referrals and your earnings can cover your costs from day one.

Two separate balances. Automatic re-entry and automatic upgrade spend only your in-matrix total above — the crossing reserve plus earnings the system already holds for you. Money sitting in your wallet cannot be used automatically; it is only spent when you press Self Rescue or Upgrade yourself. So a full wallet does not stop you being parked.
⏳ Automatic re-entry is running — you do not have to do anything. If your in-matrix balance covers the entry fee, the system re-enters you automatically at no cost to you. If it falls short, the Stability Fund can advance the gap for you — that is a repayable advance that stays on your account until it is cleared, not a gift. You can also act yourself with Self Rescue or Copay below — please read the note underneath first.
⚠️ A rescue does not always end in a seat. When both halves of your tier’s pair are completely full, a rescue can take the payment and still leave you parked, because there is no free seat to hand you. We found this from a member who paid six times and was never seated once — that was our fault, not his, and not yours. While your pair is full, paying again is unlikely to change the outcome. The fix is built and tested and goes out with the next contract release.
Self Rescue (No Debt): Pay only the shortfall from your wallet. Your withdrawable covers the rest. No SF loan — nothing is owed back.
Copay Rescue (Instant Loan): No wallet funds needed. Your reserve and earnings go in first — the Stability Fund lends only the remaining gap. The loan is a repayable advance that stays on your account until cleared, repaying automatically from your future earnings.
Why a tier can ask again right after you rescue it. Every tier is a pair — Matrix A and Matrix B — and the figure‑8 moves you between them. Clearing one half seats you there; the system then moves you on to the other half, and if your in‑matrix balance does not cover that entry, you park there too. So T1 Matrix A can appear moments after you cleared T1 Matrix B. That is a new seat, not a second charge for the one you just cleared — each rescue only ever takes that one position's shortfall, and every position is listed separately below with its own numbers.
Why the approval is bigger than the shortfall. Self Rescue asks your wallet to approve the full entry fee, but only your shortfall is ever taken — the rest stays approved and unspent. The approval is deliberately the larger figure so that a quote which moves while you are signing cannot leave you approved for slightly too little to re-enter.
